Rules & Regulations

All students must follow these rules when booking and using KK10 rooms.

🏢 Room Reservations

  • Keep your Booking ID as a reservation reference.
  • Applicants are responsible for any damage, loss, or incidents occurring during the borrowing and usage period.
  • Ensure the room is clean after use.

⚠️ General Reminders

  • Ensure all reservation details are accurate before submission.
  • Follow the reserved date and time.
  • If you wish to cancel a reservation, please contact the PIC on duty.
  • If there is a clash and your need to use the room is urgent or important, please contact the PIC in charge.

Your cooperation is greatly appreciated in ensuring the proper and smooth use of KK10 facilities. Thank you! 🤝
