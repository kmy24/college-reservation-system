Rules & Regulations

All students must follow these rules when booking and using KK10 facilities.

🏢 Room Reservations

  • Applicants must inform the PIC on duty to arrange key collection.
  • Keep your Booking ID as a reservation reference.
  • Keys must be returned after use.
  • Applicants are responsible for any damage, loss, or incidents occurring during the borrowing and usage period.
  • Ensure the room is clean after use.

🏸 Court Reservations

  • Keep your Booking ID as a reservation reference.
  • Students may proceed to use the court based on their reservation.
  • In the event of overlapping reservations, please refer to the PIC for confirmation.

⚠️ General Reminders

  • Ensure all reservation details are accurate before submission.
  • Follow the reserved date and time.
  • If you wish to cancel a reservation, please contact the PIC on duty.
  • Using any facility, whether a room or court, without a reservation is prohibited. Further action will be taken by the KK10 management.

Your cooperation is greatly appreciated in ensuring the proper and smooth use of KK10 facilities. Thank you! 🤝
